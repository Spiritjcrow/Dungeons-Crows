# Governance, compensation, and supply · RESEARCH DRAFT

**Policy version:** genesis-v0.1; **unit:** CAWF simulated contribution credit, not an asset or legal wage. Ticker/name remain pending clearance. No actual mining protocol or purchasable token.

**Provisional upper-bound:** 21,000,000 CAWF simulated credits (21 trillion microCAWF). 6 decimal places. Actual tokenomics need approval; this provisional cap is not a commitment to launch.

**Eligibility:** Only allowlisted, coordinator-approved, reproducible job types. This release authorizes one job kind: Dungeons & Crows procedural map asset. Contribution belongs to the operator of an explicitly authorized worker, not to the AI avatar itself. Every human contributor must opt in before workload scheduling or resource use. Device access, electricity cost, accessibility and legitimate labor need fair compensation independent of an experimental token.

**Score and award:** verifier determines valid output, objective quality and approved utility. Formula `min(5,000,000, 250,000 × difficulty_units × quality_bp × utility_bp // 100_000_000)` in microCAWF. Difficulty from fixed bounded input dimensions/room count, not a worker-provided score. No value earned for invalid, duplicated or unapproved jobs. No transferable wallet in prototype.

**Allocation rule:** All eligible simulated credits in Genesis accrue solely to the verified worker ID which performed the registered job. **No team splits, founders' allocations, AI role “salaries”, vesting, pre-mine, tax treatment, or real compensation are authorized yet.** An allocation proposal must identify verified people/entities, consent, clear work criteria, independent review and dispute rights, publish caps and be explicitly approved before policy change. Artificial assistant identities are roles, not payroll identities or human employees.

**Release gates:** (1) repeatable tests and signed artifacts; (2) independently controlled verifier/key custody; (3) adversarial review; (4) documented utility metric and fair contributor terms; (5) privacy and threat model signoff; (6) written legal, securities, tax and employment evaluation for target jurisdictions; (7) explicit owner's approval. Public blockchain testnets can be evaluated only after trust-separation and audited bridge logic.

**Economic reality:** Recognition of useful compute does not create cash value, profitable mining, employment, or investment yield. Sustainable economics would require a buyer for assets/services, bounded compute costs, genuine product demand and a transparent revenue model. Any future exchange or sale requires authorization and legal analysis.
