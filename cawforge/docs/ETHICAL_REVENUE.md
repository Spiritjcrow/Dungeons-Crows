# Ethical monetization and shared stability charter (draft, not adopted)

**Product rule:** Dungeons & Crows is free to download and play. Any optional paid character cosmetic, character customization, themed pack, supporter badge, or bonus is **$4.99 USD or less per paid SKU**. Game progression must be fair without buying extras. No real-money pay-to-win, hidden recurring billing, loot-box odds without clear disclosure, or false scarcity. The player always retains a playable free experience. Explicit user approval is required for pricing exceptions, new billing methods and promotion campaigns.

**Economic separation:** Stripe/Play Billing collect fiat revenue from approved premium items; CAWF is separate technical research, with nontransferable, no-cash-value simulation credits. No claims of investment returns, wages, token value, conversion rate, or imminent withdrawals.

**First practical launch:** release an enjoyable small playable free chapter, ship one or two completed cosmetic assets, use sandbox/test tracks first, verify purchase delivery/revocation and a clear support/refund contact, then request Jessica's approval for live merchant activation and actual payment collection. Deliver value before soliciting purchases.

**Budget before distributions:** transparently count actual settled gross receipts, taxes, platform/payment fees, refunds/chargebacks, hosting and accessibility/support costs, then calculate true net after costs. Contributor pay, owner distributions, and community assistance must never be calculated from gross as though it were available cash. Track who contributed, under what agreement, and approve human allocations expressly. Never auto-pay AI workers or issue real crypto tokens.

**Support others:** only propose an optional community support budget from real, sustainably positive net income, with consent, measurable purpose, recipient privacy, and public totals without exposing private donor/recipient identities. Neither charity nor income is guaranteed. Social impact should never substitute for fair compensation or proper accounting.

**Business metric dashboard:** track free installs, meaningful active players, conversion to paid extras, average paid transaction, completed/refunded sales, cash fees, net actually settled, support tickets, chargebacks and time-to-fulfillment. Put an explicit `sandbox` vs `live` marker on all charts and exports. No fake revenue or inflated CAWF value.

**Approval gates:** Selma Black implements; Selma White tests; Sol Blue examines security; Sol Red performs authorized threat tests; Sol UltraViolet compiles reviews; the account owner approves all live sale, payout and allocation changes. This charter is only a proposal until all affected people consent.
