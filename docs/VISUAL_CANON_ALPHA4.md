# Dungeons & Crows — Alpha 4 Visual Canon

This document is the binding art-direction reference for the production client.

## Screen language
The game should read immediately as a cinematic gothic action RPG rather than a web dashboard:
- full-screen rendered world;
- corvid player character physically present in the scene in third person;
- first-person option for direct immersion;
- red Resolve/health orb and blue Crow Essence/magic orb;
- compact ability hotbar at the lower center;
- enemy/boss health and identity near the upper combat field;
- minimap and current objective in the upper-right region;
- restrained party/chat overlays;
- menus over live or cinematic world imagery rather than neutral application panels.

## Character direction
Corvid humanoids are grounded fantasy warriors, not mascots:
- anatomically convincing raven/crow heads and plumage;
- layered plate, mail, leather and cloth;
- practical belts, pouches, weapons and relic slots;
- readable class silhouettes;
- feathers integrated with armor movement and wind;
- dark materials with controlled metallic highlights.

## World direction
Primary locations:
- Ravenwatch-style moonlit fortress/city.
- Siege battlefronts with fire, banners, troops and destruction.
- Wide mountain/valley regions with castles, rivers, forests and bridges.
- Torchlit multi-level crypts and dynamic dungeons.
- Snow/wet-stone corruption zones with persistent InkMesh-like traces.
- Multiplayer camp/ruin spaces with visible party members.

## Effects direction
Effects should expose state, not obscure it:
- sparks and weapon trails on resolved attacks;
- shield/feather distortion on guard;
- black-feather and violet/ember ritual effects;
- fog and lighting shifts during world morph;
- debris and dust when architecture moves;
- footprints, splashes, ash, snow displacement and persistent marks where supported;
- strong silhouettes remain readable through every effect.

## Morphic environment rule
Environment morphs may be dramatic, but are driven only by committed canonical state. Geometry, terrain, lighting and weather are rendering consumers of state, never alternate authorities.

## Non-negotiable
The flat grey panel prototype is a development harness only. It is not a visual reference for the finished game.
